# Smoothing

When a control jumps (an automation step, or a modulation change), each engine follows it the way its original module would: some smooth it, some don't.

- **Macro Oscillator:** Harmonics, Timbre, Morph, Frequency, Decay and LPG Colour follow Plaits' knob filter: Timbre, Morph, Decay and LPG Colour settle in about 25 ms (time constant), Harmonics and Frequency in about 50 ms. Model switches at once. Aux Mix is EG13's own control; a 5 ms fade removes clicks.
- **Digital Oscillator:** no smoothing, as on the hardware (Braids reads its knobs directly). Fast automation of Timbre or Color can therefore zipper.
- **Modal Synth:** Elements' firmware filters. Most controls have a time constant of about 50 ms; Flow, Mallet and Geometry are a little slower, and Position and Space take about half a second to settle. Model and Ext In switch at once.
- **Resonator:** Rings' knob filter (time constant about 50 ms) on Structure, Brightness, Damping, Position and Frequency (so Frequency glides briefly). Model, Exciter and Polyphony switch at once. Spread is EG13's own control; a 5 ms fade removes clicks. A new note's velocity (the Resonator's level) also fades in, with a time constant of 5 ms.
- **Audio Input:** Gain has a 1 ms fade. Attack and Release are times, so they need none.
- **Analog Filter:** the VCV module reads its knobs every sample; EG13 moves Frequency and Resonance in a straight line across each control block of up to 16 samples (a block ends with the 16-sample tick, or earlier where a host buffer ends), so a step glides over at most a third of a millisecond at 48 kHz. Mode switches at once.
- **Classic Filters:** as in Surge, the filter's coefficients glide: each 16-sample control block they move a fifth of the way to a new target, in a straight line across the block, so a step settles in a few milliseconds (a time constant of about 4.5 blocks, 1.5 ms at 48 kHz). Model switches at once.
- **State Variable Filter:** Cutoff and Resonance move in a straight line across each control block of up to 16 samples (a block ends with the 16-sample tick, or earlier where a host buffer ends), as in the Macro Oscillator's own filters. Mode switches at once.
- **Ring Mod / Vocoder:** Warps' own knob filter, once per 60-sample block. Algorithm, Timbre and both Levels have a time constant of about 25 ms; Osc Pitch about 6 ms. Within each block Warps glides its parameters itself. Carrier, Modulator, Osc Shape and Mode switch at once. As on the module, the very first 0.6 ms after a reset glides inside Warps from its start-up settings.
- **EQ:** the VCV module reads its knobs every sample; EG13 moves all ten knobs in a straight line across each control block of up to 16 samples (a block ends with the 16-sample tick, or earlier where a host buffer ends). Input Pad and Output switch at once.
- **Envelope:** no smoothing, as on the module: its controls are read every 8 samples of its own 31.25 kHz (a quarter of a millisecond). Mode switches at once.
- **LFO:** Tides glides Frequency, Shape, Slope, Smoothness and Shift itself across each of its 8-sample blocks (an eighth of a millisecond). Mode, Output, Range and Sync switch at once.
- **Random / S&H:** no smoothing, as on the module: controls are read every 5 samples of its own 32 kHz. X Steps, below its middle, smooths the random values themselves.
- **Function Generator:** no smoothing, as on the module: controls are read every 4 samples of its own 48 kHz. Mode and Waveform switch at once.
- **Reverb (Part FX):** Elements' knob filter, once per 16-sample block at the reverb's 32 kHz: Amount, Time, Diffusion and Brightness settle with a time constant of about 50 ms. Freeze switches at once.
- **Granular (Part FX):** no smoothing beyond Clouds' own, as in the VCV port, which passes its knobs straight through: controls are read every 32 samples at the effect's 32 kHz (1 ms). Clouds itself glides Blend across each of those blocks and holds each grain's Position, Size and Pitch for the life of the grain. Freeze switches at once; Mode and Quality fade out and in over 10 ms each (see [Part FX](part-fx.md#granular)).
- **Granular Delay (Part FX):** the module's knob filter: every control except Freeze, Quality and Seed passes the module's knob law (Pitch's intervals, the Random controls' dead zone at 50 %) and then a low-pass once per 4 kHz block (a quarter of a millisecond), settling with a time constant of about 5 ms, whatever the Quality. The module's small hysteresis on its Time knob is not reproduced. Freeze and Seed switch at once; Quality fades out and in over 10 ms each (see [Part FX](part-fx.md#granular-delay)).
- **Modulation:** EG13 works out the routes every 16 samples (a third of a millisecond at 48 kHz) and moves the Amp, the pan and the gains in a straight line across those 16 samples, so modulating them does not zipper. Routes into an engine's controls reach that engine every 16 samples and are smoothed as listed above for that engine. A Filter or Shaper gets each such change at the sample where its sound arrives, and the ones that glide across a control block (State Variable Filter, Analog Filter, Classic Filters, EQ) glide over the rest of that 16-sample tick; when a host buffer ends first, the glide ends with it. So renders at different buffer sizes can differ: by about 1 % of the level at most, briefly, during a fast change, with host buffers under 16 samples, and far less, if at all, with longer ones. Without an Amp route the Amp opens and closes over 5 ms.

After a reset, or when a voice starts from silence, every control starts at its current value with no glide (except inside the Ring Mod / Vocoder, as noted above); the Amp starts closed and opens as described in [Modulation](modulation.md).
